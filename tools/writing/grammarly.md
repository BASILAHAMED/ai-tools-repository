# Grammarly

**Developer:** Grammarly Inc.  
**Category:** Writing & Content  
**Website:** https://grammarly.com  
**Pricing:** Freemium - Free, Premium ($12/mo), Business ($15/user/mo)

---

## 📋 Overview

Grammarly is the world's leading AI-powered writing assistant, used by millions of professionals, students, and teams. It provides real-time grammar, spelling, punctuation, and style suggestions across all platforms. With a 4.7/5 G2 rating from over 11,000 reviews, Grammarly has become essential for anyone who writes in English.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Real-time Grammar & Spelling** - Instant corrections as you type
- **Style & Tone Suggestions** - Adjust writing for audience and purpose
- **Clarity & Conciseness** - Improve readability and eliminate wordiness
- **Plagiarism Checker** - Detect plagiarism and generate citations
- **Brand Voice** - Maintain consistent brand tone across teams
- **Tone Detector** - Identify and adjust writing tone
- **Genre-specific Suggestions** - Tailored for academic, business, creative, etc.
- **Cross-platform** - Browser extension, desktop apps, mobile keyboards
- **Microsoft Office Integration** - Works in Word and Outlook
- **Team Features** - Style guides, analytics, admin controls

## 🎯 Best For

- Business communication (emails, reports, proposals)
- Academic writing and research papers
- Content creation (blogs, articles, social media)
- Non-native English speakers
- Teams requiring consistent brand voice
- Students and educators
- Customer support and help desk agents

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Primarily focused on English (limited multilingual support)
- Can be overly prescriptive for creative writing
- Premium features required for advanced suggestions
- Occasionally misses context-specific nuances
- Privacy concerns for sensitive documents

## 📚 Pro Tips

- Use **tone adjustments** to match your audience (formal, casual, confident, etc.)
- Create **team style guides** for consistent brand voice
- Enable **plagiarism checker** for academic and professional work
- Use the **browser extension** for real-time suggestions across websites
- Leverage **clarity suggestions** to improve conciseness
- Set **goals** (audience, formality, intent) for better suggestions
