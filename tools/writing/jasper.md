# Jasper AI

**Developer:** Jasper (formerly Jarvis)  
**Category:** Writing & Content  
**Website:** https://jasper.ai  
**Pricing:** Creator ($49/mo), Pro ($125/mo), Business (custom)

## 📋 Overview

Jasper AI is a leading AI writing assistant specifically designed for marketing teams and agencies. It focuses on brand voice training, campaign creation, and SEO-optimized content. With strong team collaboration features and a vast template library, Jasper helps businesses create consistent, high-quality marketing content at scale. It's particularly popular among content marketers, copywriters, and social media teams.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Brand Voice Training** - Teach Jasper your unique brand tone and style
- **Campaign Creation** - Generate entire marketing campaigns from brief
- **SEO Optimization** - SurferSEO integration for keyword optimization
- **50+ Templates** - Blog posts, ads, emails, social media, product descriptions
- **Team Collaboration** - Real-time collaboration and approval workflows
- **Content Calendar** - Plan and schedule content
- **Plagiarism Checker** - Integrated Copyscape for original content
- **Chrome Extension** - Use Jasper anywhere on the web
- **Multi-language** - 30+ languages supported
- **Analytics** - Content performance tracking
