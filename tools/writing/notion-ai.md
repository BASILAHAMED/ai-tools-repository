## 📋 Overview

Notion AI is an integrated AI assistant within the popular Notion workspace platform. It brings AI capabilities directly into your notes, documents, databases, and wikis. Seamlessly blending with Notion's existing features, it helps with writing, summarization, brainstorming, and automation. For Notion users, it's a natural extension that enhances productivity without leaving the workspace.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI Writing & Editing** - Generate, rewrite, and improve content
- **Summarization** - Condense long documents and meetings
- **Brainstorming** - Generate ideas and outlines
- **Translation** - Translate content to multiple languages
- **Database Automation** - AI-powered database operations
- **Meeting Notes** - Process and summarize meeting notes
- **Q&A** - Ask questions about your workspace content
- **Template Generation** - Create page templates with AI
- **Custom Prompts** - Save and reuse custom AI prompts
- **Slash Commands** - Quick access with `/ai` commands
