# Gemini

**Developer:** Google (DeepMind)  
**Category:** AI Assistants & Chat  
**Website:** https://gemini.google.com  
**Pricing:** Free, AI Premium ($19.99/mo)

---

## 📋 Overview

Gemini is Google's flagship multimodal AI assistant, deeply integrated with Google Workspace and Google's ecosystem. Formerly known as Bard, Gemini offers seamless access to Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and other Google services. With 2.47% global AI chatbot market share, it's a strong competitor, particularly for users already in the Google ecosystem. Its real-time information access and cost-effective API pricing make it attractive for developers and enterprises.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Google Workspace Integration** - Deep integration with Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive
- **Multimodal Capabilities** - Text, image, and video understanding
- **Real-time Web Search** - Always-on internet access
- **Gemini Advanced** - Premium tier with more powerful models
- **Mobile App** - Dedicated iOS and Android apps
- **Extensions** - Connect to Google services and third-party apps
- **Coding Assistance** - Code generation and debugging
- **Image Generation** - Powered by Imagen (in some regions)
- **API Access** - Google AI Studio and Vertex AI
- **Context Window** - Up to 1M tokens (Gemini 1.5 Pro)

## 🎯 Best For

- Google Workspace users
- Research with real-time information
- Gmail and Docs productivity
- Android integration
