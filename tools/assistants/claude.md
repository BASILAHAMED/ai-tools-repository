# Claude

**Developer:** Anthropic  
**Category:** AI Assistants & Chat  
**Website:** https://claude.ai  
**Pricing:** Freemium - Free, Pro ($20/mo), Team ($30/mo)

---

## 📋 Overview

Claude is Anthropic's flagship AI assistant, known for its emphasis on safety, reliability, and superior performance on complex tasks. With a 200K token context window (largest in the market at launch), Claude excels at long-form content creation, technical documentation, and structured analysis.

## ✨ Key Features

- **200K Token Context** - Process extremely long documents and conversations
- **Artifacts** - Shareable content workspace for collaborative editing
- **Projects (Library)** - Create projects with custom data and knowledge bases
- **Constitutional AI** - Built-in safety and alignment principles
- **Multi-modal** - Text and image understanding
- **Claude Code** - CLI tool for coding assistance
- **File Upload** - Support for multiple file formats

## 🎯 Best For

- Professional writing and long-form content
- Technical documentation and reports
- Code generation and documentation
- Structured analysis and data interpretation
- Complex problem-solving with step-by-step reasoning
- Teams requiring high reliability and safety

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Lower message limits even on paid plans
- Burns through tokens quickly with large files
- Less multimodal than some competitors
- Occasional over-cautiousness due to safety training

## 📚 Pro Tips

- Use **Projects** to build custom knowledge bases for specific domains
- Leverage **Artifacts** for collaborative document creation and editing
- Upload large documents (PDFs, codebases) for comprehensive analysis
- Use Claude for **technical writing** and documentation
- Take advantage of **step-by-step reasoning** for complex problems
