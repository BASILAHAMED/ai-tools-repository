## 📋 Overview

Fathom is an AI meeting assistant that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings. It integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, providing instant summaries, action items, and transcriptions. With a perfect 5/5 G2 rating from over 5,200 reviews, Fathom is the highest-rated meeting AI tool and is used at over 180,000 companies.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Automatic Recording** - Records meetings with one click
- **Real-time Transcription** - Live transcription with speaker identification
- **AI Summarization** - Automatic meeting summaries with key points
- **Action Item Extraction** - Identifies tasks and follow-ups
- **Multi-language Support** - Transcribes in 20+ languages
- **Shareable Clips** - Create and share meeting highlights
- **CRM Integration** - Sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive
- **Notion Integration** - Push summaries to Notion
- **Slack Integration** - Share summaries in Slack channels
- **Searchable Transcripts** - Full-text search across all meetings
