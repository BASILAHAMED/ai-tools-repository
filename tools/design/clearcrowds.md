## 📋 Overview

ClearCrowds is an AI photo cleanup and editing app for removing unwanted people, objects, clutter, glasses, watermarks you have rights to remove, shadows, and other distractions from images. It is designed for everyday photo edits as well as lightweight creator, real estate, product-photo, and travel-photo workflows.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Object and Crowd Removal** - Remove people, clutter, and distracting items from photos
- **Preset-Based Editing** - Use focused cleanup presets for common photo problems
- **Natural-Language Edits** - Describe the change you want when a preset is not enough
- **Before/After Review** - Compare edits before downloading or continuing work
- **Mobile-Friendly Workflow** - Supports app-style photo cleanup on the go

## 🎯 Best For

- Travel photos with crowds or unwanted background objects
- Real estate and rental images with visual clutter
- Product photos that need quick cleanup before publishing
- Portrait cleanup such as glasses, glare, shadows, or small distractions
- Creators who want narrow, repeatable AI edits instead of a full design suite

## 🔧 Technical Details

- **Platform:** Web and mobile app entry points
- **Primary Use Case:** AI photo cleanup and object removal
- **Workflow:** Upload a photo, choose a preset or prompt, generate, compare, and export

## 🏆 Competitive Advantages

1. **Focused Photo Cleanup** - Built around practical edit scenarios rather than broad image generation
2. **Preset Library** - Helps users start from common cleanup tasks quickly
3. **Simple Review Loop** - Encourages comparing the result before export

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Very large overlays, document marks, or edits over important text can be unreliable
- Rights-sensitive edits such as watermark removal should only be used on owned or authorized images
- Complex professional retouching may still require a manual editor
