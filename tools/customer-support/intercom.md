## 📋 Overview

Intercom Fin is Intercom's AI-powered customer support bot, designed to handle customer inquiries, answer questions, and resolve issues automatically. As part of the Intercom customer messaging platform, Fin provides human-like responses, integrates with knowledge bases, and seamlessly escalates to human agents when needed. It's trusted by thousands of businesses for 24/7 customer support automation.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Natural Conversations** - Human-like responses and understanding
- **Knowledge Base Integration** - Pull answers from your help docs
- **Multilingual Support** - 40+ languages
- **Context Awareness** - Understands conversation history
- **Smart Escalation** - Smooth handoff to human agents
- **Custom Workflows** - Build custom support flows
- **Analytics & Insights** - Track performance and improve
- **A/B Testing** - Test different responses and approaches
- **Customization** - Branding and tone matching
- **API Access** - Integrate with existing systems
