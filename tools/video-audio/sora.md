## 📋 Overview

OpenAI Sora is a revolutionary text-to-video generation model that creates cinema-quality videos from simple text descriptions. Announced in early 2024, Sora can generate highly realistic, coherent scenes with smooth motion, consistent characters, and impressive physical accuracy. It represents a major leap forward in AI video generation and is expected to transform film, marketing, education, and content creation industries.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Text-to-Video Generation** - Create videos from text prompts
- **Cinema-Quality Output** - Professional-grade visuals and motion
- **Smooth Motion** - Physically accurate and fluid animations
- **Character Consistency** - Maintain consistent characters across scenes
- **Multiple Resolutions** - Up to 1080p and beyond
- **Long Duration** - Generate videos up to 60 seconds (and expanding)
- **Scene Complexity** - Handle complex scenes with multiple elements
- **Photorealism** - Near-photorealistic video generation
- **Camera Motion** - Control camera movements and angles
- **Style Control** - Various visual styles and aesthetics
