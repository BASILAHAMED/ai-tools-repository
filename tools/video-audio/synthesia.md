## 📋 Overview

Synthesia is the leading AI video generation platform, allowing users to create professional videos with AI avatars and voiceovers without cameras, actors, or studios. It's used by 60% of Fortune 100 companies for training videos, marketing content, and communications. With 140+ AI avatars in 130+ languages, Synthesia democratizes video production for businesses of all sizes.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI Avatars** - 140+ realistic AI presenters
- **130+ Languages** - Global language support
- **Text-to-Speech** - High-quality, natural-sounding voices
- **Template Library** - 60+ video templates
- **Screen Recording** - Record your screen with avatar overlay
- **Custom Avatars** - Create personalized AI avatars (Enterprise)
- **Brand Kits** - Maintain brand consistency
- **Collaboration** - Team workspaces and approvals
- **Analytics** - Video engagement tracking
- **Integrations** - PowerPoint, Google Slides, LMS platforms

## 📚 Pro Tips

- Write **clear, conversational scripts** for natural delivery
- Use **short sentences** for better avatar timing
- Choose avatars that match your **audience and brand**
- Leverage **templates** for quick production
- Use **custom avatars** for brand consistency (Enterprise)
- Record **screen demos** with avatar for tutorials
- Add **subtitles** for accessibility and engagement
- Test different **voices** for best match
